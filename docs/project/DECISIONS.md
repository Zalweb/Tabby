## DEC-009 ? Explicit consent for expense changes

Date: 2026-11-02. Source: approved CAP-006/LED-005 scope and integration-owner implementation review. Registered expense participants require every other registered participant to approve before balances change, including group expenses. An unclaimed manual contact has no app identity: its owner may record a reasoned change only after explicitly attesting an agreement outside Tabby. The UI and audit state that this is an owner attestation, not an authenticated contact approval. Claimed contacts cannot use that path. Alternatives were deferring all contact corrections until claiming, or treating a local owner action as contact authentication; the former leaves existing manual records uncorrectable and the latter would misrepresent consent. All flows retain originals/payments and append compensating records. Affected contracts: CAP-006 request_expense_change_v2, reviewer/decision audit, correction UI and cached revision metadata. This does not authorize money movement or alter DEC-004/005.
