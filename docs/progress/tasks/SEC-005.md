## Scope and acceptance

[Audit finding](../../audits/2026-10-31-android-review.md#aud-20261031-027). Primary owner role: Security agent.

- Problem: Privacy, export and deletion journeys are absent.
- Impact: Users cannot understand access or manage their data.
- Implement: Add accurate privacy notice, caller-scoped export and reauthenticated deletion request; retain shared financial audit records with anonymization.
- Acceptance: Export has no unrelated data; deletion request is tracked and does not destroy confirmed shared records.
- Starting point: [source](../../../lib/features/profile/presentation/profile_screen.dart).
