## Scope and acceptance

[Audit finding](../../audits/2026-10-31-android-review.md#aud-20261031-007). Primary owner role: Ledger/UI agent.

- Problem: Group payments change uninvolved balances.
- Impact: Bob paying Carol incorrectly changes Alice balance.
- Implement: Preserve recipient in fetch/model/cache and apply payments only to the payer and recipient.
- Acceptance: Bob-to-Carol payment leaves Alice unchanged on server, cache, UI and after refresh.
