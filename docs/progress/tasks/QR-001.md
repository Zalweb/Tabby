## Scope and acceptance

[Audit finding](../../audits/2026-10-31-android-review.md#aud-20261031-013). Primary owner role: Payments agent.

- Problem: Account-string QR is presented as a payment QR.
- Impact: Payers may receive an unusable wallet destination.
- Implement: Remove generic text QR generation from receiving-method setup; upload wallet-exported receiving QR.
- Acceptance: Setup offers upload and rejects non-QR images; no generic account-number QR appears.
- Starting point: [source](../../../lib/features/payment_methods/presentation/payment_methods_sheet.dart).

## Implemented

Current result (2026-11-01T05:29:31.732Z): Dated superseding correction: the earlier receiving-method sheet fix left a separate account-number generator in profile completion. That duplicate editor/generator is now removed. Both profile setup and owner management use the same decoded-QR validation/editor, which accepts wallet-exported images and never generates a payment QR from account text. Setup labels entries as drafts until server acknowledgment; manual text details remain distinct from QR images. Prior immutable object/access rules remain.
