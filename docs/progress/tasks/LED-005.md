## Scope and acceptance

[Audit finding](../../audits/2026-10-31-android-review.md#aud-20261031-005). Primary owner role: Data/sync agent.

- Problem: Contact payer and settlement paths lose synchronization.
- Impact: Local success can fail to reach other devices or survive reinstall.
- Implement: Support contact PartyRef in atomic expense and manual receipt operations; never silently discard a rejected operation.
- Acceptance: A contact can pay or owe; expenses and partial cash settlements persist through refresh/restart.
- Starting point: [source](../../../lib/features/tabs/application/tabby_providers.dart).

## Implemented

Current superseding result (2026-10-31T13:31:47.752Z): Atomic expense validation supports owned contact payers and debtors in private bilateral tabs. Cash records retain actual payer/recipient PartyRefs separately from the recording owner, enforce outstanding amount under a tab lock and actor/operation idempotency. Client/cache/parser preserve parties and local/server tab identity; contact UI exposes explicit cash recording. Balance RPCs use actual parties. Rejected operations remain queued for attention.
