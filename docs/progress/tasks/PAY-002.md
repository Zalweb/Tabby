## Scope and acceptance

[Audit finding](../../audits/2026-10-31-android-review.md#aud-20261031-020). Primary owner role: Payments/backend agent.

- Problem: Submit Payment Proof does not attach payment proof.
- Impact: The form has no proof and later attachment targets a transaction.
- Implement: Attach optional proof/reference to the payment ID and authorize recipient access; preserve offline uploads.
- Acceptance: Payer submits proof offline/online and recipient sees it on the same payment.
- Starting point: [source](../../../lib/features/tabs/application/tabby_providers.dart).

## Implemented

Current result (2026-10-31T18:25:52.891Z): Payment form offers optional image proof and reference. The writer captures bytes into the actor-scoped durable queue before local success, preserves the original payment ID and pending status, and submits registered payments with atomic submit_payment_v2 proof metadata. Later payment attachments use an append RPC on the payment ID, not transaction receipt update. Dependent local targets are explicitly remapped before parent acknowledgment. Contact cash proof append has its own frozen command and stays queued until attached. Recipient can read the exact private object; strangers cannot. Referenced proof objects cannot be overwritten/deleted by client roles.
