# Tabby 🐾

> **"Keep tabs. Settle up."**

**Tabby** is a friendly, socially frictionless financial tracker designed for friends, roommates, and social circles in the Philippines. It makes tracking shared expenses, peer-to-peer debts ("utang"), reminders, and payment settlements effortless, clear, and stress-free.

---

## Key Features

- 🇵🇭 **Philippine Peso First (`₱`):** Centavo-accurate ledger math designed for local spending habits.
- 👥 **Shared Tabs & 1:1 Balances:** Track who owes whom without awkwardness or confusion.
- 🍗 **KKB & Bill Splitting:** Instant division of dining tabs, including tax and service charge distribution.
- 🐱 **Expressive Mascot Companion:** Visual emotion feedback celebrating settlements and gently disarming debt anxiety.
- 📱 **Seamless Settlement Reference:** Quick payment tagging for GCash, Maya, Bank Transfer, and Cash.
