3. NON-NEGOTIABLE TABBY PRODUCT CONTRACTS

Every repair and feature must preserve:

Money and identity:
- Ledger amounts and shares are integer centavos.
- Use immutable user/contact identity and PartyRef contracts.
- Never merge people or deduplicate expenses solely by display name, amount, or date.
- Maintain one bilateral relationship per immutable pair.
- Groups contain parties; a group is never a payer or recipient.
- Group obligations retain their actual payer, recipient, participants, and allocations.

History and consent:
- Confirmed financial history is append-only.
- Corrections/cancellations preserve originals, reasons, payments, and required audit visibility.
- Follow DEC-009 participant approval and explicit outside-app agreement rules for unclaimed contacts.
- Unfriending does not delete obligations.
- Contact conversion/claiming requires explicit consent.

Payments:
- Tabby does not move money.
- Saved wallet-issued receiving QR → external payment → explicit submission → recipient verification/confirmation → balance change.
- Displaying/scanning a QR, uploading proof, or submitting payment never automatically confirms receipt.
- Confirmation/rejection operates on the existing submission.
- Preserve payer and recipient identities, immutable proof revisions, and receipt/proof distinctions.
- Never generate a plain account-string QR and represent it as a wallet-issued payment QR.

Authorization:
- Receiving methods are available only to their owner, accepted friends in the permitted profile context, and actual debtors in payment flows.
- Server authorization is mandatory regardless of UI visibility.
- Registered accepted friends are eligible group members; unregistered contacts remain bilateral tab-only parties.

Synchronization:
- Preserve durable operation IDs, actor-scoped idempotency, payload conflict detection, and serialized financial writes.
- Persist commands before networking and reconcile results before acknowledgment.
- Network availability does not establish successful synchronization.
- Results from an old account/session generation must not update a later session.
- Signed media URLs are temporary views of durable object references.

Product presentation:
- Preserve the approved theme, PHP/English M1 scope, calm conversational language, and no emoji UI copy.
- Do not introduce speculative functionality, payment processing, unsupported preferences, or misleading capability claims.
