Privacy Policy

We believe you deserve to know exactly how your information is handled. This policy explains everything clearly.

Effective Date: September 18, 2026
Short version: Tabby collects only what is necessary to provide you with a shared expense tracking service. We do not sell your data, and we do not process or hold actual money on your behalf.

1. Who We Are

Tabby ("we," "our," or "us") is a peer-to-peer financial relationship tracker built for Filipino barkadas, roommates, and friend groups. Tabby is developed and operated by Zalweb, based in the Philippines.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: privacy@tabby.app

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and optionally your profile photo.
  • Google Sign-In: If you use Google Sign-In, we receive your Google account email and display name. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Payment Contact Details: You may voluntarily save your GCash phone number, Maya account number, or a QR Ph image to your profile. These are used solely to help friends settle debts with you inside the app.
  • Expense Records: Descriptions, amounts (in Philippine Peso centavos), dates, and participants for each expense or payment you log.
  • Receipt Images: Photo attachments you voluntarily upload as proof of payment.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and app version for crash reporting and compatibility purposes.
  • Usage Data: General feature usage patterns (which screens you visit, how often you log expenses) to improve the app. This data is anonymized and aggregated.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your Tabby account.
  • To display your running Tab ledger between you and your friends.
  • To allow friends you have added to view your payment details (GCash, Maya, QR Ph) for settlement purposes.
  • To send you in-app notifications about tab activity (reminders, payment confirmations).
  • To diagnose bugs, fix crashes, and improve app performance.
  • To comply with legal obligations under Philippine law.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

4. Sharing Your Information

We only share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Friends You Add: Your display name, profile photo, and payment details (GCash/Maya/QR Ph) you have saved are visible to friends you have explicitly added in the app.
  • Service Providers: We use Supabase (database and authentication infrastructure) and Vercel (web hosting). These providers process your data solely to deliver the service under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by Philippine law, court order, or government authority.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in Supabase's PostgreSQL database hosted in a secure cloud environment. We implement the following safeguards:

  • Row Level Security (RLS) — you can only read your own data and tabs you are a participant of.
  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit.
  • Encrypted storage for sensitive fields (GCash number, Maya number, QR Ph images).
  • Append-only ledger design — financial records are never silently deleted or overwritten.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and expense data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate business purposes (such as dispute resolution).

7. Your Rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Object: Object to our processing of your data for specific purposes.
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@tabby.app.

8. Children's Privacy

Tabby is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

9. Third-Party Links

Tabby may display links to GCash, Maya, or other third-party services for settlement convenience. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external services. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via an in-app notice or by updating the effective date above. Continued use of Tabby after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us
