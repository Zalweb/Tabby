Questions, answered

Good to know before you split.

Straight answers about money, privacy, and the way Tabby works.

Is Tabby free to use?

Yes. Tabby is free for you and your friends. There are no subscription fees, and Tabby does not take a cut of settlements.

Does Tabby hold or move my money?

No. Tabby is a record of what you and your friends confirm. You settle directly through GCash, Maya, QR Ph, or cash, then mark the payment in the tab.

How do splits work?

Split a bill equally, or enter a custom amount for each person. Tabby stores every amount as an integer number of centavos so totals stay exact.

What happens when a friend pays?

They can attach a receipt as proof. The payment only clears the tab after the recipient reviews and confirms it.

Can I use Tabby offline?

Yes. Expenses you log without a connection are queued durably on your device and synced when you are back online.

Can I track group trips and shared households?

Yes. Group tabs keep one shared ledger with per-person balances for trips, households, and barkada events.

Which payment methods are supported?

You can save GCash, Maya, and QR Ph details or a QR image to your profile. Friends you have added can view them when it is time to settle.

Is my data private?

Yes. Row-level security limits every record to you and the friends in that tab, and sensitive payment fields are encrypted in the database.
